#4471 · two causes of wrong booking dates

owner support desk triaged reviewed 2026-11-22 · 0 days ago

The reports in the original incident describe bookings on the wrong date. Two parts of the booking flow can cause that symptom. They need separate fixes.

What the desk found

  • Shared parser. The parser reads the month first even when the tenant writes the day first. It can interpret an intended 7 April as 4 July. Read the date in the tenant's format.
  • Booking-v3 date hint. The form tells people to enter MM/DD/YYYY even when it reads their input day first. A customer following that hint can type 04/07/2026 for 7 April and get 4 July. Make the hint match the format the form accepts.
